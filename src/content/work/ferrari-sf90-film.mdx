## The film

*SF90 Stradale: The Beginning of a New Era. Reimagining performance, elegance, and innovation.*

A remake of Ferrari's official SF90 Stradale launch film. It opens with a studio reveal that brings the car out of darkness, then follows a driver onto the road at dusk, through a tunnel and across a bridge in low sun. The picture is framed at 2.39:1.

## Stills from the film

Frames from the 4K master, in running order. Select an image to view it larger.

### Studio reveal

The car appears one light at a time: the headlights first, then a rim light along the roofline and a red backlight, then close-ups of the headlight, intakes, shield, tail lights and brakes before the full profile. I lit this sequence.

<Boards set="ferrari-sf90/studio" items={studio} columns={3} lead />

### Dusk

A driver in a red race suit arrives at dusk, helmet in hand. The camera stays close to the car: the mirror, the SF90 Stradale badge, the visor, the instrument cluster and the sky in the roof. Then the car is out on the highway. Environment lighting by Zedong Lin.

<Boards set="ferrari-sf90/dusk" items={dusk} columns={3} lead />

### Tunnel

The car launches inside a tunnel with smoke off the rear tyres, followed by chase shots and close-ups of the wheel and the yellow brake caliper.

<Boards set="ferrari-sf90/tunnel" items={tunnel} columns={3} lead />

### Bridge

The last sequence runs along a coastal highway and across a bridge in low sun, with tracking shots from the road and from the air.

### End titles

The SF90 Stradale title and the Ferrari prancing horse close the film, followed by the credits.

## Lighting study

The film's own shots with every material replaced by matte grey, so only the lighting is left. I rendered them again with Lumen's lighting-only pass in Unreal Engine 5.8 and matched them to the frames of the film. The video plays the two side by side: the lighting-only render on the left and the final film on the right.

### Studio lighting

The reveal is built from rect lights that come on shot by shot. Without the paint, each one shows as a single soft gradient across the body.

### Driving scenes

Outside, the car is lit by the sky and a low sun. With the reflections gone it reads much darker than in the film, because most of its brightness there is the paint mirroring the sky. Environment lighting by Zedong Lin.
