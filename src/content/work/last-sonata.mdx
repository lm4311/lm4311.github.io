## The concept

### Humanity's revival begins here

In **Last Sonata**, a zombie survival FPS inspired by modern AAA apocalyptic titles, the entire experience unfolds within one vast, interconnected map. You play as Sam, a retired military officer, partnered remotely with Iris, a commander providing overwatch from above.

Guided by the final hint left by a mad scientist, Sam and Iris must locate the Five Elemental Keys hidden throughout a residential village. It is a test of coordination, survival instincts and firepower.

## My contribution

### Solo development

As the solo creator, I serve as both programmer and designer. I built the shooting mechanics, enemy behaviors, traversal systems and the user interface entirely in Unreal Engine 5.

My focus was on game feel: tuning weapon recoil, scripting multi-phase boss battles, and optimizing performance (LODs, object pooling) to keep the action fluid.

## Key outcomes

- **Immersive combat:** system-driven combat with clear attack cues and cinematic transitions.
- **Advanced AI:** horde AI using NavMesh and NavLinks for complex pathfinding and crowd queuing.
- **Polish:** dynamic crosshairs, motion-warped traversal and polished UI feedback.
